## Brand & Style

This design system channels an elevated arcade aesthetic tailored for modern high-output productivity. It merges the dopamine triggers of high-end tactile gaming with the sleek discipline of contemporary software design.

- **Brand Personality**: Exuberant, empowering, precise, and celebratory. The interface operates like a well-calibrated physical game console: responsive, weighted, and deeply rewarding.
- **Target Audience**: Digital creators, ambitious builders, and gamers seeking tangible momentum and gamified focus without childish tropes or visual clutter.
- **Aesthetic Movement**: Tactile Modernity. It pairs crisp, light surfaces and generous whitespace with soft pill containers, vibrant spectral accents, inner surface highlights, and extruded physical depth on interactive triggers.
- **Design Principles**:
  - *Every Action Has Mass*: Interactive elements feature subtle physical resistance and multi-layered depth rather than flat, weightless planes.
  - *Dopamine by Design*: Progression cues (streaks, XP gains, coin balances) leverage luminous amber and emerald accents against disciplined slate architecture.
  - *Clarity Over Novelty*: Gamified elements enhance orientation and completion velocity without obscuring core task execution.

## Colors

The palette relies on an energetic electric indigo primary, calibrated against a high-contrast neutral slate base and luminous status accents.

- **Primary (`#6366F1`)**: Electric Indigo. Serves as the primary focus anchor, brand signature, primary CTA surface, active state indicator, and level-progress fill.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Radiant Amber. Reserved for XP gains, virtual economy/coin balances, achievement stars, and celebratory milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Hyper Emerald. Dedicated to streaks, quest completions, verified health stats, and forward momentum.
- **Urgent / Hazard (`#F43F5E`)**: Punch Coral. Used selectively for critical boss encounters, imminent deadlines, streak loss warnings, and destructive actions.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) creates crisp contrast against elevated white surfaces.
  - `Card Surface`: `#FFFFFF` for primary work areas and container modules.
  - `Muted Surface / Wells`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate-100) for unearned progress tracks, badge sockets, and input troughs.
- **Borders & Dividers**: High-definition `#E2E8F0` (Slate-200) boundaries with selective translucent highlights on interactive items.
- **Typography & Icons**: `#0F172A` (Slate-900) for commanding body and heading copy, down to `#64748B` (Slate-500) for metadata and XP denominators.

## Typography

The typography utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project a clean, technical, yet friendly geometry.

- **Headlines**: Tight negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) paired with heavier weights (`700` and `800`) ensures high impact without visual bulk.
- **Labels & Micro-Indicators**: Styled in `800` weight with subtle positive letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) when set in uppercase, producing crisp arcade-style telemetry.
- **Numeric Values & XP Counters**: Leverages tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure streak counters, coin values, and timer readouts remain geometrically stable during increments.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an adaptable 12-column responsive grid centered within fixed maximum-width shells to preserve ergonomic task arrangement.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Max container bound is 1280px. Main task track occupies 8 columns, while telemetry, quest logs, and player stats occupy 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Sidebar transitions into collapsible action shelves.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) edge margins. Stat summaries condense into a top horizontal scroll strip.
- **Rhythm**: Internal spacing strictly observes an 8-point base rhythm, stepping down to 4px for badge tags, micro-pills, and interactive indicators.

## Elevation & Depth

This system utilizes tactile extrusion and diffused ambient light to simulate physical surfaces that invites clicking, tapping, and dragging.

- **The Extruded 3D Edge**: Key action drivers (buttons, reward chests, primary cards) use a solid bottom shadow ring (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #4F46E5` for indigo triggers, `0 4px 0 0 #D97706` for amber rewards) paired with a 1px border. On active click, the element translates down 2px (`transform: translateY(2px)`) while the bottom extrusion ring compresses to 2px.
- **Base Ambient Shadows**:
  - `Level 1 (Resting Tiles)`: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
  - `Level 2 (Floating Popovers & Modals)`: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Gloss & Specular Sheen**: Badges and high-tier achievements feature an internal 1px semi-transparent top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to evoke a glossy physical token finish.
- **Backdrop Blur**: Modal sheets and sticky tracking bars leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over an 85% opacity surface.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a hyper-smooth pill-oriented aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`) to sustain an inviting, approachable atmosphere.

- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Standard for action buttons, filter tags, search bars, counter chips, streak tags, and progress bar hulls.
- **Modules & Work Surfaces**: Base cards, dialogs, and quest blocks adopt `rounded-xl` (`3rem` or `24px` clamped on mobile) to frame content softly without eating usable reading area.
- **Sockets & Insets**: Gauge wells and tracker cutouts mirror the perimeter radius of their containers to maintain consistent concentricity.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Fully rounded pill. Solid `#6366F1` fill, crisp white typography (`label-lg`), with a `#4F46E5` 3D bottom edge. Includes a subtle top inner-rim light.
  - *Secondary / Gamified Gold*: `#F59E0B` fill with `#D97706` bottom extrusion for claiming bounties, leveling up, or converting tokens.
  - *Ghost / Subtle*: Pill outline using `#E2E8F0` border, transparent background, and `#0F172A` text; shifts to `#F1F5F9` on hover.
  - *Click Dynamics*: All solid buttons use a tactile spring-down effect (`translate-y-[2px]`) on active state.

- **Chips & Badges**:
  - Pill-shaped status indicators with an 8px circular glowing status pip.
  - *Streak Chip*: Amber-tinted background (`#FEF3C7`), `#B45309` typography, accompanied by a miniature flame icon.
  - *XP Counter Chip*: Rich slate background (`#0F172A`), white stat text, accompanied by an amber coin icon.
  - *Category Pill*: `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text, transitioning to solid primary indigo upon selection.

- **Checkboxes & Habit Toggles**:
  - Habit complete targets utilize a 24px circular hit area with a 2px slate border (`#CBD5E1`).
  - *Completion State*: Instant transition to `#10B981` fill with an animated white check icon, accompanied by a momentary radial particle ring or ring-pulse animation.

- **Input Fields**:
  - Pill or softly rounded containers (`rounded-2xl`) using `#FFFFFF` fill, enclosed in `#E2E8F0` border.
  - *Focus State*: 2px solid `#6366F1` outer glow ring with zero layout shift (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`).
  - Floating prefix slot for quest icons, tag markers, or urgency selectors.

- **Cards & Quest Blocks**:
  - White surface enclosed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with 20px internal padding.
  - Left edge features an integrated priority color band (3px width: Indigo for regular, Emerald for streak, Coral for critical).
  - Subtle hover lift: `-2px` vertical translation paired with deep indigo ambient shadow (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`).

- **Progress Bars & XP Tracks**:
  - *Outer Well*: Capsule container with a recessed inner shadow and `#F1F5F9` fill.
  - *Fill Bar*: Gradient fill (`#6366F1` to `#818CF8` for XP; `#10B981` to `#34D399` for daily completion) featuring rounded ends and a subtle diagonal gloss shimmer animation upon level completion.
